Active Research Direction

Data Quality & Domain Specialization Beat Raw Scale at Small Parameter Counts

At the 160M–1B parameter scale, training loss and downstream syntax coherence depend exponentially more on aggressive AST deduplication, structural fill-in-the-middle mixture, and strict provenance filtering than on raw token volume. Pythia-160M serves as the experimental vehicle to systematically quantify this inflection point.
