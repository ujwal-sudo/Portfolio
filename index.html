01 ABOUT

About Me

I'm Ujwal — an AI engineer and final-year Electronics & Telecommunication student at SGGSIE&T, Nanded. My route here ran through robotics clubs, cameras, football and code before it narrowed, deliberately, to one question: how do intelligent systems actually work?

These days I train small language models, build agentic systems, and document what I learn as I go — because calling a model is easy; understanding it is the interesting part.
